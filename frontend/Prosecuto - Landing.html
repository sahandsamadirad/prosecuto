01 / Overview   

A red light camera ticket is owner liability — no points, no suspension. Prosecuto turns that quiet advantage into a real defence: it interviews you, finds your grounds, and rehearses you until you can hold your own in court.

02 / Two ways
Two paths to dispute

Every ticket can be challenged two ways. Prosecuto knows the difference and prepares you for the one you choose — or both.

Path A

Early Resolution

You meet a prosecutor before trial to negotiate the charge. Informal, no Justice of the Peace, no oath. You speak only to the Crown.

  • Charge withdrawn
  • Fine reduced
  • Amended to a lesser offence
Path B

Trial

A formal hearing before a Justice of the Peace under the Provincial Offences Act. Evidence is tendered, objections are valid, proof is beyond a reasonable doubt.

  • Crown opens, then the defence
  • Examination & cross-examination
  • Closings, then verdict

03 / Lawyer Mode · Alex

A coach who builds your case with you

Alex interviews you about the ticket, the intersection, and who was driving — then determines your defence grounds and produces a preparation package, walking you through it section by section.

  • AOpening statement — exactly what to say to open your case.
  • BDefence script — an ordered script of what to say and when.
  • CAnticipated questions — likely asks from the Crown or JP, with answers.
  • DEvidence & disclosure — what to gather and what to formally request.
  • EDelivery coaching — tone, pacing, and the correct form of address.

04 / Judge Mode · Mock trial

Rehearse the whole trial before it's real

A Justice of the Peace, a Crown prosecutor, and a court clerk run the full Provincial Offences Court sequence — strictly, in order. You speak your part. At the verdict, the AI breaks character and reviews your performance.

  • ★Procedurally exact — the JP enforces the sequence and rules on objections.
  • ★Real evidence package — Certificate of Offence, photograph, camera operating certificate.
  • ★Honest feedback — specific moments from the transcript, what to fix.
