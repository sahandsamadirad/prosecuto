Fine Amount: Payment amount ordered by the court or voluntarily prepaid by the person charged, excluding fee, court cost, victim fine surcharge, and collection agency costs.
Fines Collected:  Payment amount made in a year by the person charged, excluding fee, court cost, victim fine surcharge, and collection agency costs. HTA fines data is not available by specific offence. Fines collected in a year may represent fines imposed in prior years.
Fine Amounts in Default: Amounts include the fine amount that went into default, excluding fee, court cost, victim fine surcharge payable and collection agency costs. 'Fine Amounts in Default' do not include unpaid fines that have not yet been noted as being in default and also do not include unpaid fines that have been written-off by Municipal Partners.
